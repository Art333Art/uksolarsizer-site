EV and home energy

V2L, V2H, V2G and a removed pack are not synonyms

They differ in electrical boundary, equipment, grid interaction and whether the battery remains in the road vehicle.

Last reviewed: 16 August 2026

The four concepts

TermEnergy goes toWhat must support it
V2LPortable appliances through a vehicle-provided outlet/adapterVehicle’s designed V2L function and rated connection
V2HA building electrical systemCompatible bidirectional vehicle, charger/power converter, changeover/control and compliant installation

Connector does not prove capability

CHAdeMO has supported bidirectional projects, including UK trials with Nissan Leaf vehicles. CCS-family bidirectional capability is developing, but a connector shape alone says nothing about a specific vehicle, firmware, charger, supplier or UK service. Never assume an arbitrary EV can power a home.

Why V2L is not V2H

V2L normally supplies isolated appliance loads within the vehicle manufacturer’s power and connection rules. Connecting that output into household fixed wiring creates different changeover, earthing, protection and backfeed questions. A socket adapter does not turn V2L into an approved house supply.

V2H and V2G boundaries

Bidirectional power conversion may sit in the vehicle or charge point. V2H coordinates with the building; V2G also exports to the network. The GOV.UK smart-charging action plan describes V2X as the umbrella and notes that most smart charging was one-way while bidirectional availability was expected to grow. Availability must therefore be verified at purchase time.
