Use one denominator

Calculate purchase £/nominal kWh, purchase £/estimated usable kWh, then complete £/usable kWh. Include every component needed to create the same functional boundary: battery through inverter, protection, containment and installation.

Illustration A cheap EV pack can lead on the first metric and lose on the third. Conversely, a technically sound large pack paired with an already-compatible inverter may retain an advantage. Without dated quotes and measured SOH, declaring either route cheapest is speculation.

Avoid chemistry slogans

LFP generally trades lower energy density for strong cycle-life and thermal-stability characteristics, while NMC/NCA offer high energy density. Pack-level risk also depends on cell condition, mechanical protection, sensing, fault interruption, thermal propagation design, installation and controls. “LFP safe / NMC unsafe” is not an engineering assessment.

A practical choice process

  1. Define required delivered kWh and kW.
  2. Choose low-voltage or high-voltage inverter architecture.
  3. Obtain documented battery limits, condition and compatibility.
  4. Cost the complete protective and control chain.
  5. Compare expected delivered lifetime energy, warranty and serviceability.
